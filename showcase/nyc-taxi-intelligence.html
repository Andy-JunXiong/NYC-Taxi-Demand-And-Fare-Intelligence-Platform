CHAPTER FIVE · WHAT THIS DOESN’T CLAIM

Trust also means naming the boundary.

Not all urban mobility

Yellow Taxi records do not represent rideshare, transit, walking, cycling or every NYC traveller.

Not causal inference

Calendar patterns describe governed demand. They do not prove why a neighbourhood or hour is busy.

Limited external context

Weather is optional and major event knowledge is not yet a comprehensive real-time feed.

Not dispatch optimisation

The output supports planning and review; it does not assign individual vehicles or guarantee revenue.
